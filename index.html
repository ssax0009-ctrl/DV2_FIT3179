SOURCES & METHODS

Built from published Australian data.

  • AIHW, Australian Atlas of Healthcare Variation Time Series: Hysterectomy hospitalisations, 2014–15 to 2021–22. Supplied workbook; source notes identify the National Hospital Morbidity Database and ABS estimated resident populations.
  • State and territory boundaries: geoBoundaries gbOpen Australia ADM1, sourced to the Australian Bureau of Statistics. ABS copyright and Creative Commons terms.

Hysterectomy rates cover women aged 15 and over and are age-standardised to the 2001 Australian population. Endometriosis rates are crude rates reported by AIHW. The ACT did not provide private-hospital data for 2024–25.
