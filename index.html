TERMS TO KNOW

A short glossary.

AIHW
Australian Institute of Health and Welfare, the national agency that publishes health and welfare statistics.
Age-standardised rate
A rate adjusted to account for differences in age between populations, making comparisons fairer.
Crude rate
The number of events relative to a population, without adjusting for its age profile.
Endometriosis
A condition where tissue similar to the lining of the uterus grows elsewhere in the body.
Hysterectomy
An operation to remove the uterus.
LHN
Local Hospital Network: a group of public hospitals and health services serving a region.
Median wait time
The middle wait time: half of the patients waited less, and half waited longer.
SA3
Statistical Area Level 3, a geographic area used by the Australian Bureau of Statistics to report regional data.
SES
Socioeconomic status: a way of describing social and economic circumstances, such as relative advantage or disadvantage.
Suppressed data
Figures not published by the data source, often to protect privacy or because estimates are unreliable.
